Pick your adventure

Safety first. The phone lives in a pocket while anyone moves. After every tag the app says to put the phone away, and a few seconds later the screen locks itself so nothing gets tapped by accident. Take the phone out only when you reach the next tag. Never run or climb with it in your hand.

Think games are about clues and puzzles. Run games are about speed, so the phone stays in the pocket the whole time.
